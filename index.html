FAQ

Why is the stack of Baxilib and Dragons Heaven secret?

Both run on the private XI engine, whose technology is not disclosed publicly. The buyer receives the full source code and the stack details under an NDA. What we can say publicly: the database is PostgreSQL 17, nothing else is required on the server (no Redis, no search server, no message broker), it ships as a Docker image, and it comes with an automated test suite.

Are the platforms the same product in different frameworks?

No. Each is a separate working product with its own strengths. Use the comparison table and each platform's "Good to know" section.

Can I change the interface language?

NineHeavens and Dragons Heaven are in English. Baxilib is in Russian with a built-in English switch (about 80% of strings translated). Weba, Solomonster and erIIIba are in Russian with hard-coded strings. Translation is available as an add-on service.

Is hosting included? Are there monthly fees?

All platforms are self-hosted on your own server. The license is a one-time payment. Support and updates can be agreed separately.

Can I resell the code?

No, unless your contract explicitly allows it. Redistribution, resale or public publication of the source code, sublicensing and removal of licensing restrictions are prohibited by default.
